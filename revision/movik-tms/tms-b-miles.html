Questions dispatchers ask.

How do my carriers join?

Add them by USDOT. If they're already on Movik, the owner gets a request and decides what you see. If not, you set them up and send them a link to claim it.

Can Miles go below my number?

No. You set the floor and Miles negotiates up from there. He will not go below your number.

Does the driver see the rate?

No. The driver gets a link with the stops, the times and your number. Never the rate.

What happens with the rate con?

Upload it and Movik fills in the load. Then you assign it, dispatch it and track it until it's paid.
