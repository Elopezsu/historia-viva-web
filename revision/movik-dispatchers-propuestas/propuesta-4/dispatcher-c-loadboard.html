Questions dispatchers ask.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Movik TMS is free for dispatchers right now. You can create your account today.

Does it work with my load board?

Yes. Install Miles in Chrome and open DAT One or Truckstop. Every load you see shows up in Movik with the broker checked.

Do I have to install anything?

Only Miles, a Chrome extension, to bring in your load board. Everything else runs in your browser.

Do my carriers have to pay?

No. You invite them by DOT number and they get a free Movik account.

Can I earn for the carriers I bring?

Yes. Through the Movik Partner Program you earn extra income for every carrier you refer. Ask about it and our partner team will walk you through it.
