Movik TMS · Dispatcher onboarding

Walkthrough of tms.movik.us on Nov 18, 2026, as a new dispatcher, synced with the Nov 17 meeting (Leo, Klayton, Juan Fernando, Eunice). Tags: Agreed In progress Proposal

Four actions for this week
  1. Email-only sign up. Ask for the phone later, in Settings. Test it for 2–3 weeks.
  2. Gmail without the scare. Google review in progress; until then, warn before the click and land on the Email tab after connecting.
  3. A shorter checklist, in the right order. Merge the duplicated driver step and let people import carriers in bulk.
  4. Fix the small stuff. Tour skips steps, one carrier still asks to pick a carrier, two names for the same button, empty fee field.

1 · Sign up Agreed

Today it takes 6 steps before the dispatcher sees the product:

  1. Name
  2. Company name
  3. Work email and mobile phone
  4. Password with 5 rules + terms
  5. SMS code ("Check your phone")
  6. Into the TMS ("Welcome to Movik TMS, add your first carrier"; no plans for now)
  • Remove the phone from sign up and send the code by email. Leo agreed on Nov 17. Most of it already exists: the "Didn't receive the SMS? Try email instead" path and the "Confirm your email" message. The phone moves to Settings › You.
  • Measure it for 2–3 weeks: sign ups started vs. completed, before and after. If it doesn't help, it goes back.
  • Proposal Put name, company and email on one screen (3 steps → 1). "Your own name works too" already says the company can be skipped.
  • Proposal Password: keep "8+ characters" only, or move to a magic link later.
  • Proposal Step 2: the "Your company invited you to Movik" card looks like a button. Label it "Preview" or make it lighter.

2 · Connecting Gmail In progress

Gmail shows "Google hasn't verified this app … Go to Movik Email (unsafe)". Movik submitted the app for review (about 10 days). Outlook and the other providers don't show it. Connecting does work once you click through: the mailbox loads.

  • Before the Google button, one line: "Google will show a notice while we finish their review. Click Advanced, then Go to Movik Email."
  • After connecting, land on Inbox › Email with "Gmail connected ✓". Today it lands on Messages (Telegram), and it looks like nothing happened.
  • Until Google approves, list Microsoft first and keep "Other email (IMAP)" visible.

3 · First minutes inside: checklist and tour Agreed

The checklist (Today › All steps)

"Invite your drivers" (step 3) opens /dashboard/drivers, the same page as "Add a driver" (step 5), and its text ("Send a driver their first load sheet") repeats "Send a load to your driver". Proposed order, 9 steps instead of 10:

#StepNote
1Add or import your carriersPoint to "Import carriers" too: paste USDOT/MC numbers or a CSV, checked with FMCSA.
2Invite the carrier's ownerKeep.
3Add a truckOr connect the ELD.
4Add a driverName and phone is enough.
5Book a loadDrop a rate con, or book it from the load board.
6Send a load to your driverReplaces "Invite your drivers".
7–9Optional: Install Miles · Connect Gmail or Outlook · Offer e-mailKeep as optional. Gmail already ticks itself when connected.

Show the referral block ("Know another dispatch company?") after the first delivered load, not on day one.

The guided tour ("Take the tour")

  • Bug: it opens on "2 of 5" (Loads), Next jumps to "4 of 5" (Inbox), and "5 of 5" (Settings) renders off screen at the bottom.
  • Content (Juan Fernando): the first stop is Add carrier + dispatch fee, because the fee is what turns on gross, net and "Your fees" on Today.
  • Suggested stops (4, not 20): Add carrier + fee → Loads (drop a rate con) → Load board (install Miles) → Inbox (connect email). Leo's idea fits here: Miles can narrate it.

4 · Load board In progress

The load board is the feature the consultancy said was missing, and it now works through the Miles extension (the same extension as Miles). Pending, per Leo: the screen that tells the user to install it.

  • "Your boards" already has the right screen ("DAT One opens here with Miles", Add Miles to Chrome / Check again). Reuse it as the empty state of Load board › Search, which today only says "One search across your boards".
  • Add Miles, DAT One, Truckstop and the ELD to Settings › Integrations. Today it only lists Email and Telegram.
  • Landings and emails now say it: "Your load board, inside your TMS" (proposals 3 and 4).
