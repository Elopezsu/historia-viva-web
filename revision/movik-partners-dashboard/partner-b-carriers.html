Partner questions.

Who can be a partner?

Anyone in the trucking industry carriers trust: dispatchers, other carriers, repair shops, the people who help them run their business.

The bonus is paid when your carrier funds their first load with Movik.

How much can I earn?

It depends on how you work with carriers. Apply and our partner team will walk you through it.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining the Partner Program is free.
