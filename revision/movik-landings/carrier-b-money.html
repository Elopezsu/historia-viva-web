Before you sign up.

How does the advance work?

You get up to 40% of the load before pickup, and the rest when the proof of delivery clears. The service fee is shown before you accept.

Is Movik free?

The TMS is free to use: broker search, fleet, routes, documents and your numbers. Advances have a service fee that depends on the load.

Can I switch from my current provider?

Yes. Tell us when your agreement ends. We review it with you and plan the move for that date, so you never get caught in the middle.

Do I sign a long contract?

No. If it doesn't work for you, you stop. No penalty.

Do I have to finance all my invoices?

No. You choose which invoices to finance and which ones to keep. Most factors make you finance 100%. With Movik it's your call.

What do I need to sign up?

Your DOT or MC number, your name, phone and email. Movik fills in your business details from your number. To unlock advances you verify your authority, bank account and ID once.
