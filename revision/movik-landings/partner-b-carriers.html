Partner questions.

Who can be a partner?

Dispatchers and anyone carriers trust: the people who help them run their business.

When do I get paid?

The bonus is paid when your carrier funds their first load with Movik. The monthly share is paid while they keep working with Movik.

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining the Partner Program is free.

What do my carriers get?

A free Movik account, broker search, and the option to get paid before pickup once verified.
